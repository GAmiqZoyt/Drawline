Privacy Policy

DrawLine ("the App") is developed by Cortexlab Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App collects and how it's used. DrawLine works fully offline for gameplay, and we collect the minimum data needed to run the app and its ads.

Information We Collect

DrawLine does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, or any personally identifiable information.

  • Game progress (level reached, IQ scores, achievements) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Advertising data: DrawLine shows optional rewarded video ads through LevelPlay (Unity/ironSource) mediation, which may serve ads from multiple ad networks. These networks may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), approximate location, and app usage data to serve and measure ads.

How We Use Information

Advertising data collected by our ad mediation partners is used solely to display and measure the performance of in-app ads. We do not sell your data or share it with any third party outside of our advertising partners.

Children's Privacy

DrawLine is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization").
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game progress from your device.

Data Retention

We do not operate servers that store your gameplay data — it lives only on your device. Advertising data is retained by our ad partners according to their own retention policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
