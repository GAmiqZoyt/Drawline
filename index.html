How it works

One rule. Endless variations.

No tutorials to sit through. The whole game is one rule, and every level is a new way to test it.

01

Start on the glowing dot

Every level has one or two valid starting points. Find one, put your finger down.

02

Trace every line, once

Drag through the whole tangle without lifting your finger. Cross every line exactly once.

03

Lift early, start over

No hints, no available-path highlighting. Plan the route in your head before you commit.
